Android app. Play your music offline.

Download for Android

About 156 MB. Use Wi-Fi if you can.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If Chrome says the file may be harmful, tap Download anyway. That message shows for any app that is not from the Play Store.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open on the download notification, or open it from your Files or Downloads app.
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for Chrome, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
Download stops or fails? Check you have about 400 MB of free space, stay on one network, and try again. Chrome will resume it from the Downloads list.
Updating? Install the new file over the old app. Your account and downloaded songs stay.
Prefer to send it to your phone? Copy the link and paste it into WhatsApp or a browser on the phone.
